📋 Rules & FAQ

🎯 Objective

Pocket all 8 balls (excluding the cue ball) in any order and into any pocket in the fastest time possible.

🎱 Setup

  • Standard 9-ball rack (or 8-ball rack with one ball removed)
  • Break from behind the head string

⏱️ Gameplay

Breaking:

  • Timer starts when the cue ball is struck on the break

Shooting:

  • Pocket all 8 object balls in any order into any pockets
  • No need to call shots
  • Continue shooting until all balls are pocketed
  • Timer stops when the final ball is legally pocketed

Scratches:

  • Any scratch (on break or during play) results in a +5 second penalty
  • Place the cue ball within a corner pocket (player's choice) for the next shot
  • Continue playing - scratches do not end your attempt

⚠️ Penalties (added to final time)

  • Missed shot: +2 seconds
  • Scratch (any time): +5 seconds, cue ball placed in corner pocket for next shot

⭐ Bonuses (subtracted from final time)

  • Bank shot: -2 seconds per bank (ball contacts at least one rail before pocketing) - Press the button once for each bank in the shot
  • Combination shot: -1.5 seconds (cue ball strikes one object ball which pockets another)
  • Perfect round: -5 seconds (complete the round with no missed shots - automatically applied when stopping timer)

🏆 Scoring

Final time = Raw time + Penalties - Bonuses

Lowest time wins!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

📱 How do I add this app to my home screen?

On iPhone/iPad (iOS):

  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome or other browsers)
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right corner
  5. The 🎱 icon will appear on your home screen!

On Android:

  1. Open this page in Chrome
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
  4. Confirm by tapping "Add" or "Install"
  5. The 🎱 icon will appear on your home screen!

🎥 How do I screen record my shootouts?

On iPhone/iPad (iOS):

  1. Open Settings → Control Center
  2. Add "Screen Recording" if not already added
  3. Swipe down from the top-right corner (or swipe up from bottom on older iPhones) to open Control Center
  4. Tap the record button (⚫ inside a circle)
  5. Wait for the 3-second countdown
  6. Open JD's Shootout app and start playing
  7. To stop: Tap the red status bar at the top, then tap "Stop"
  8. Video saves automatically to your Photos app

On Android:

  1. Swipe down from the top to open Quick Settings
  2. Swipe down again to see all options
  3. Look for "Screen recorder" or "Screen record"
  4. If you don't see it, tap the pencil icon to add it
  5. Tap the screen recorder icon
  6. Select your preferences (sound, touches, etc.)
  7. Tap "Start"
  8. Open JD's Shootout and start playing
  9. To stop: Swipe down and tap the recording notification, then tap "Stop"
  10. Video saves to your Gallery or Photos app

Note: Some older Android devices may require downloading a screen recording app from the Google Play Store.

📹 Why isn't my camera working?

The camera feature requires HTTPS (a secure connection). If you're seeing an error:

  • Make sure you've granted camera permissions when prompted
  • Try refreshing the page and allowing permissions again
  • Ensure no other app is currently using your camera
  • If accessing via file:// (local file), the camera won't work - you need to access it via a web server or add it to your home screen first

💾 Will my data be saved?

Yes! All your settings, player names, and game state are saved locally on your device using browser storage. Your data stays private and never leaves your device.

🔄 How do I switch between Solo and VS Mode?

Simply tap the "Solo" or "VS Mode" buttons at the top of the controls section. In VS Mode, you can customize player names by tapping on the text fields.
