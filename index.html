📋 JD's Billiard Shootout Rules

🎯 Objective

Pocket all 8 balls (excluding the cue ball) in any order and into any pocket in the fastest time possible.

🎱 Setup

  • Standard 9-ball rack (or 8-ball rack with one ball removed)
  • Break from behind the head string

⏱️ Gameplay

Breaking:

  • Timer starts when the cue ball is struck on the break
  • If the cue ball is scratched on the break, player receives a +5 second penalty and must place the cue ball within a corner pocket (player's choice) for the next shot

Shooting:

  • Pocket all 8 object balls in any order into any pockets
  • No need to call shots
  • Continue shooting until all balls are pocketed or a scratch occurs
  • Timer stops when the final ball is legally pocketed

⚠️ Penalties (added to final time)

  • Missed shot: +2 seconds
  • Break scratch: +5 seconds, cue ball placed in corner pocket for next shot
  • Scratched cue ball (during play): Attempt ends immediately

⭐ Bonuses (subtracted from final time)

  • Bank shot: -2 seconds per bank (ball contacts at least one rail before pocketing) - Press the button once for each bank in the shot
  • Combination shot: -1.5 seconds (cue ball strikes one object ball which pockets another)
  • Perfect round: -5 seconds (complete the round with no missed shots - automatically applied when stopping timer)

🏆 Scoring

Final time = Raw time + Penalties - Bonuses

Lowest time wins!
